How Nexora Agency handles information

Draft for review · Prepared October 2026

This is a starter policy draft for the website prototype, not legal advice. Customize it with accurate business details and review it for applicable laws before accepting real customers or payments.

1. Information you provide

When you contact Nexora or request a service, you may choose to provide your name, email address, project details and messages needed to respond to your request. Do not send passwords, full payment-card details or other highly sensitive information through general forms.

2. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, prepare project proposals, provide requested services, communicate about orders, maintain account security and improve the website.

3. Storage and service providers

The planned account system may use Firebase services for authentication and data storage after it is configured. The current prototype is not connected to a live Firebase project, so its forms should not be treated as a functioning account or submission system.

4. Sharing and security

Information should only be shared with service providers or people who need it to operate the service, or where disclosure is required by applicable law. Reasonable security measures should be used, but no online service can promise absolute security.

5. Retention and your choices

Keep personal information only as long as needed for the purpose it was collected for or to meet legitimate recordkeeping requirements. You may contact Nexora to ask about access, correction or deletion, subject to applicable requirements.

6. Children and privacy

The service is not intended to collect personal information from children without appropriate authorization. If you believe a child has submitted personal information improperly, contact the site operator.

7. Policy updates and contact

This draft may be updated as the service changes. Before launch, add the agency’s verified contact email and have this policy reviewed for the laws that apply to your business.
