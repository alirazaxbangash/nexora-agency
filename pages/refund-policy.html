Policy
A transparent starting point for cancellations and refunds

Draft for review · Prepared October 2026

This is a starter policy draft for the website prototype, not legal advice. Customize it with accurate business details and review it for applicable laws before accepting real customers or payments.

1. Before you pay

Confirm the service scope, total price, expected timeline, deliverables and revision terms in writing before making a payment. The prototype prices and wallet are examples, not an active checkout offer.

2. Requests before work begins

If you ask to cancel before work has started, contact Nexora with your order reference. Any eligible refund should be assessed against the written agreement, payment-provider rules and applicable law.

3. Work already started

For digital services, the amount refundable may depend on work completed, approved milestones, non-recoverable third-party costs and the agreement made before work began. Nexora should explain any deductions clearly.

4. Delivery issues and revisions

If a deliverable is missing or materially differs from the agreed scope, contact Nexora with details so the team can review it and, where appropriate, offer a correction, additional delivery or another remedy under the agreement and applicable law.

5. Non-refundable items

Any non-refundable fees or expenses must be clearly disclosed and agreed before payment, and cannot override mandatory consumer rights.

6. How to request a review

Send your order reference, payment confirmation and a short explanation through the official contact channel. Never include banking passwords, PINs or full card credentials.

7. Processing and legal rights

Approved refunds should be returned using an appropriate payment method and within a reasonable timeframe, subject to provider processing times. This draft does not limit any rights you have under applicable law.

8. Important launch note

Before taking real payments, customize this policy to match the actual agency workflow, local legal requirements and payment provider terms. Add verified contact information.
