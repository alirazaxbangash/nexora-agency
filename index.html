GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, meet
clear answers.

A few helpful details before we get started.

What does Nexora Agency do?

Nexora offers digital services such as website development, brand identity, design and content support. The exact scope is agreed before a project begins.

How do I get started?

Explore the service catalogue, choose a service that fits your needs, and contact the team with your goals and any useful references.

Can I request a custom package?

Yes. Tell us about your project, preferred features, timeline and budget so the team can review the request and suggest a suitable scope.

How do orders and updates work?

Project milestones, delivery expectations and communication arrangements should be confirmed with the team before work starts. Account dashboard features will be available once the backend is connected.

How do payments work?

Payment instructions and any deposit requirements must be confirmed through official Nexora channels. Do not treat the current prototype wallet or checkout as a live payment system.

Can I ask for changes?

Revision rounds and what counts as an extra request should be agreed in the project scope before work begins, so both sides understand what is included.

How do I contact support?

Use the Get Hired/contact page to describe your request. In this prototype, forms are not yet connected to a live inbox, so submission is not guaranteed.

Where can I read the policies?

Read the Privacy Policy, Terms of Service and Refund Policy linked below. These draft policies should be reviewed and customized before the site accepts real customers or payments.
